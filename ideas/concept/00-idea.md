## Problem
DR4W / All Iron Hunt has a story, characters and concept art, but no public home. A stranger who
hears about it has nothing to open, nothing to play with and no reason to care. The game, a trailer
or a pitch will each need one place to link to.

## User
A stranger on a phone who taps a link and gives the page about ten seconds.

## Why now
The story and the character designs were just locked. The page is the first public home of the
canon, and the DR4W figure built for it is reusable for the game site and press kit later.

## Riskiest assumption
A small CSS figure of DR4W, with gear you can operate (antenna, arm cannon, core…), is enough to make a
stranger play within 10 seconds and want the game. (guess)

## Smallest test
One static page at `concept.dr4w.io`: the playable figure as the hero, the premise, chapters 1–4 with
their bosses (chapter 5 teased), and the concept art. English only. No backend logic beyond
`GET /api/health`. The full canon and design notes stay outside this public repo; only spoiler-free
text lands here.
